Customization

Selected styles can be adapted to your measurements, colour preference and select design details - subject to feasibility.

What Can Be Customized

  • Measurements and fit
  • Length
  • Colour
  • Certain design details
  • Other alterations agreed upon at the time of order

Additional charges and/or extended timelines may apply depending on the customization requested. These will be communicated and approved before production begins.

Process

How Customization Works

01  Request

Choose a piece and share your customization request via WhatsApp, email or the contact form.

02  Confirm

Our team confirms feasibility, any additional charges, and the revised timeline before production begins.

03  Create

Your piece is made to order by our atelier, with measurements and details as agreed.

04  Arrive

Your piece is dispatched with tracking details shared as soon as it leaves the atelier.

● Please note: customized orders are created specifically for you and cannot be returned or cancelled once production has commenced.
