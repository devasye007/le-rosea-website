Shipping, Returns & Exchanges

Shipping Policy

At LE ROSÈA, every piece is carefully prepared with attention to detail and quality. As our garments may involve customization and individual finishing, orders are processed with care before dispatch.

Processing & Delivery Timeline
Orders are generally dispatched within 2 weeks from the date of order confirmation and payment. Customized or specially developed pieces may require additional time depending on the level of customization. Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive the relevant shipping and tracking details.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on the destination, courier service, customs clearance, or other circumstances beyond our control. Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information, including the complete address and contact details.

Payment Options

At checkout you may pay in full online, or choose Partial COD. With Partial COD, a 50% advance is paid online to confirm the order and the remaining 50% is collected in cash at the time of delivery by the courier. The order proceeds to production and dispatch once the advance payment is confirmed.

Please note that the 50% advance payment is non-refundable if the order is cancelled after payment has been made, as made-to-order pieces begin production once the advance is confirmed.

Customization

LE ROSÈA offers customization on selected styles, subject to feasibility, including adjustments to measurements and fit, length, colour, certain design details, and other alterations agreed upon at the time of order.

Customized orders are created specifically for the customer and therefore cannot be returned or cancelled once production has commenced. Additional charges and/or extended timelines may apply and will be communicated and approved before production begins.

Return Policy

Due to the made-to-order, customized and prêt-couture nature of LE ROSÈA garments, we do not accept returns. All orders are considered final sale once confirmed.

We strongly recommend reviewing the size chart and confirming your measurements before placing an order. Our team is glad to assist with selecting the appropriate size.

Returns will not be accepted for:

  • Change of mind
  • Incorrect size selected by the customer
  • Change in colour preference
  • Customized garments
  • Minor variations that naturally occur due to handcrafted or artisanal workmanship

Exchange Policy

We offer size exchanges on eligible non-customized garments, subject to the following conditions:

  • The exchange request must be made within 7 days of delivery
  • The garment must be unused, unworn and unwashed
  • All original tags, labels and packaging must remain intact
  • The garment must not have been altered, damaged, stained, perfumed or otherwise used
  • Exchange is subject to size availability
  • Customized garments are not eligible for exchange unless the garment received has a manufacturing defect or an error made by LE ROSÈA
  • Customers are responsible for applicable shipping charges for size exchanges, unless the incorrect item or size was sent by LE ROSÈA
  • Only one size exchange per order will be permitted

How to Request an Exchange

Please contact our customer service team within 7 days of receiving your order with:

  • Order number
  • Customer name
  • Reason for exchange
  • Required replacement size
  • Photographs of the garment, if requested by our team

Once the request is approved, our team will provide the exchange instructions.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

Every LE ROSÈA garment undergoes quality inspection before dispatch. If you receive an item that is damaged during delivery, defective due to manufacturing, different from the item ordered, or different from the approved customization, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs and your order details.

After review and approval, LE ROSÈA may offer a replacement, repair, or another appropriate resolution. Claims made after the specified period may not be accepted.

Important Note

LE ROSÈA pieces are created with a strong focus on craftsmanship and individuality. Slight variations in embroidery, embellishment, colour, texture or finishing may occur, particularly in pieces involving handcrafted techniques. Such variations are not considered defects but are part of the character and uniqueness of the garment.

By placing an order with LE ROSÈA, the customer acknowledges and agrees to the terms of this Shipping, Returns & Exchanges Policy. For any questions regarding sizing, customization or your order, please contact our customer service team before placing your order.
