How to Measure

Bust

Wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape straight and level all around.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually just above your belly button. Keep the tape comfortably snug.

Hip

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your hips, keeping it straight and level all around.

Shoulder

Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other shoulder across the back, as shown in the diagram.

Use a soft measuring tape and keep it snug but not tight. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a relaxed drape - noted on each product page.
