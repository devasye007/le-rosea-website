How to Measure

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, roughly 8 inches below your waist.

Use a soft measuring tape and keep it snug but not tight. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a relaxed drape - noted on each product page.
